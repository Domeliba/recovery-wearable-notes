WHOOP Trial to Peak: Wired Charger or Wireless PowerPack?

Before converting your free trial, separate the membership features from the hardware included in your offer. The charger is a useful example.

Does converting to Peak include a wireless charger?

For a WHOOP 5.0 Peak trial, converting to an annual Peak membership means keeping the wired Basic Charger. WHOOP says a Wireless PowerPack can be bought separately. A direct 12-month Peak purchase is a different kit: its membership guide lists a wireless PowerPack.

The plan name describes your membership access. It does not guarantee that a trial conversion ships every accessory pictured with a new-member kit. Compare the kit in your own checkout before deciding.

First check which trial device you received

WHOOP 5.0 trial

The current trial FAQ lists a Jet Black CoreKnit band and wired Basic Charger.

WHOOP 4.0 trial

The FAQ also lists a 4.0 option with a Black SuperKnit band and wireless PowerPack. Hardware can be pre-owned.

WHOOP says device allocation varies by region, availability and signup route; trial members cannot choose it. Do not apply the 5.0 charger rule blindly to a 4.0 kit. WHOOP 4.0 bands and chargers are incompatible with 5.0 and MG.

My checklist before paying

  1. Identify the device: check your order confirmation and the hardware you received.
  2. Read the conversion screen: record the plan, total price, renewal date and exactly which hardware will ship, if any.
  3. Compare the total: if wireless charging or a different band matters to you, include its separate cost instead of comparing membership prices alone.
  4. Check the trial deadline: the trial can renew automatically. If you decide to leave, follow WHOOP's cancellation and return instructions for your region.
  5. Resolve a mismatch: ask WHOOP Support before paying if the displayed kit differs from your expectations. This guide cannot change an account's offer.

Where a referral fits

If you have not joined yet, compare the current referral offer and standard trial before signup. Promotions do not stack. If your trial has already started, ask WHOOP about eligibility rather than assuming a code can be added retroactively.
